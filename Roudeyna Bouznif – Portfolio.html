Wink

agent · multi-agent AI

A multi-agent platform built during my internship at Talan Tunisie, for a startup project that helps teams with research, regulation and funding.

The problem
Regulatory answers are only useful if they can be trusted, and one general-purpose agent drifts, loops and invents sources.

Football Analysis System

person · computer vision

A video pipeline that turns match footage into numbers: who is on which team, how fast they run and how far they cover.

What I built
Player detection with YOLOv8, fine-tuned on a custom dataset, and multi-frame tracking. K-Means on jersey colour assigns players to teams. Optical flow estimates camera motion so it can be separated from player motion, and a perspective transform converts pixels into real metres to compute speed and distance.
What I learned
Frame-by-frame detection is not enough on moving footage. Motion has to be modelled over time, and camera movement has to be removed first.
